Citroen C5 Tourer — model attribution
=====================================

"Citroen C5 Sedan And Estate" (https://skfb.ly/pvzsT) by Nieve5677 is licensed
under Creative Commons Attribution (http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

Modifications for DRIFTBLOCK:
  - Only the Tourer/estate body is used; the saloon is excluded.
  - The four wheels are separated onto steer/spin pivots for animation.
  - The body and wheels are decimated for real-time rendering.
  - Materials are replaced with lightweight game materials.

The generated citroen-c5-tourer-game.glb in this folder is the only model the
game loads. The original source archive stays outside the repository.
