HOW TO DRIFT

1

Build speed on the straight. Faster entry means a bigger slide — and a bigger score.

2

Turn in, then tap SPACE (handbrake) to break the rear loose. The car will rotate far more than it travels.

3

Hold the slide with W. Throttle keeps the rear wheels spinning and the drift alive. Steer into the slide to control your angle.

4

Watch the BOOST bar. The longer you hold a clean drift, the more charge you bank. Let go of the handbrake to fire a mini-turbo.

5

Your multiplier climbs every 2 seconds of unbroken drift. Straightening out banks the points. Hitting a wall burns the whole chain.

6

Got a clutch pedal? Press it, let the revs build, then dump it. The rear tyres break loose instantly — a third way in, alongside the handbrake and sheer throttle.

7

Slip angle is everything. Shallow drifts are safe; huge angles score enormously but bleed speed fast.
